🕵️ Information Gathering in Ethical Hacking

Information gathering (aka reconnaissance) is the first step in ethical hacking. It involves collecting as much data as possible about the target system, network, or company — legally and ethically.

Step 1: Passive Information Gathering

Passive methods don't touch the target directly. They avoid detection and are safer.

  • Google Dorking: Use advanced search queries to find public info (e.g., site:example.com).
  • Whois Lookup: Get domain ownership and registration details.
  • Shodan: Discover internet-connected devices like cameras and routers.
  • Social Media: Find names, emails, and positions from employee profiles.

Step 2: Active Information Gathering

Active techniques directly interact with the target and may be detected.

  • Ping Sweep: Check which systems are alive in a range of IPs.
  • Port Scanning (e.g., Nmap): Discover open ports and services.
  • DNS Enumeration: Discover subdomains and IPs.
  • Banner Grabbing: Find software versions running on services.
