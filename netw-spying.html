🕵️ Network Spying (Easy Explanation)

In this activity, you’ll learn how hackers observe what’s happening in a network. This is called network spying or packet sniffing.

Step 1: What is Network Spying?

Whenever we open a website, send a message, or play a game — small pieces of data called packets are sent over the internet. Hackers can capture these packets and read them if the network is not secure.

Step 2: Why Do Hackers Spy on Networks?

To see if someone is using insecure websites (HTTP), to find usernames and passwords, or to check which sites people are visiting.

Step 3: What Tools Do They Use?

They use tools like Wireshark or tcpdump to watch the traffic going in and out of a device.
