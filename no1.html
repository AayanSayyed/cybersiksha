Network Security Measures

Network security is crucial for protecting systems, data, and communications from cyber threats. Below are key measures to enhance network security.

1. Firewalls

Firewalls act as a barrier between trusted and untrusted networks. They help monitor and filter traffic based on predefined security rules.

  • Types of firewalls: Packet-filtering, Stateful inspection, Proxy firewalls, Next-Gen Firewalls.
  • Prevent unauthorized access by blocking malicious traffic.
  • Common firewalls: Cisco ASA, pfSense, Fortinet.

2. Intrusion Detection & Prevention Systems (IDS/IPS)

These systems detect and prevent unauthorized access or attacks in real time.

  • IDS (Intrusion Detection System): Monitors and alerts suspicious activity.
  • IPS (Intrusion Prevention System): Blocks malicious activities before they reach the network.
  • Examples: Snort, Suricata, Cisco Firepower.

3. Network Segmentation

Segmenting networks minimizes the impact of cyber threats by isolating critical assets.

  • Divides a network into smaller, controlled zones.
  • Reduces lateral movement of attackers.
  • Common techniques: VLANs, Subnetting, Zero Trust Architecture.
