Security Awareness & Training

Security awareness and training are essential in preventing cyber threats and ensuring a strong cybersecurity culture.

1. Phishing Awareness Training

Educate employees to recognize and avoid phishing scams:

  1. Learn how to identify phishing emails, messages, and links.
  2. Verify the authenticity of emails before clicking on links.
  3. Report suspicious emails to the IT department.

2. Cyber Hygiene Best Practices

Train users to follow good cyber hygiene practices:

  1. Avoid clicking on suspicious links and downloading unknown files.
  2. Keep software and antivirus programs updated.
  3. Use strong and unique passwords for each account.

3. Incident Response Drills

Regularly simulate attack scenarios to prepare employees and IT teams:

  1. Conduct simulated phishing exercises.
  2. Test incident response plans with real-world attack scenarios.
  3. Review and update security policies based on drill findings.
