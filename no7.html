Monitoring & Incident Response

Effective monitoring and incident response strategies help organizations detect and mitigate security threats before they cause damage.

1. Security Information and Event Management (SIEM)

SIEM solutions help in centralized logging and analysis of security events:

  1. Collect and correlate logs from different systems in real-time.
  2. Identify suspicious activities and generate alerts.
  3. Automate incident detection and response workflows.

2. Threat Hunting

Proactively searching for threats helps prevent potential cyber attacks:

  1. Analyze network traffic for signs of compromise.
  2. Identify unusual user behavior and system anomalies.
  3. Use threat intelligence to detect advanced persistent threats (APTs).

3. Incident Response Plan

A structured approach to responding to security incidents:

  1. Detect and analyze the scope of a security breach.
  2. Contain and mitigate the impact to prevent further damage.
  3. Recover systems and implement lessons learned to strengthen defenses.
