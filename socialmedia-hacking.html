📖 What is Social Media Hacking?

Social media hacking refers to the unauthorized access or manipulation of social media accounts or platforms like Facebook, Instagram, Twitter, etc. It’s used for stealing data, impersonation, fraud, or spreading misinformation.

🎯 Why Social Media is a Target

  • People often reuse passwords and ignore 2FA.
  • Public profiles make it easy to impersonate or trick users.
  • High engagement makes platforms great for spreading scams or malware.

💥 Common Attack Types

  • Phishing: Fake login pages collect passwords.
  • Session Hijacking: Attacker captures cookies to hijack active sessions.
  • Credential Stuffing: Using leaked data to break into accounts.
  • Social Engineering: Manipulating people emotionally to gain access.
  • Impersonation: Cloning profiles to fool friends or fans.

📌 Real-Life Examples

  • Twitter 2020 Hack: Attackers social engineered Twitter staff and posted crypto scams from Elon Musk’s and Obama’s accounts.
  • Instagram Phishing: Fake brand promotion DMs lure influencers into clicking fake login links.
  • Facebook Session Hijacking: Unsecured Wi-Fi used to steal cookies and control logged-in accounts.

🚨 Red Flags to Watch

  • Login requests from unknown devices or locations
  • Fake login pages (instagram-login.com instead of instagram.com)
  • Strange DMs with links, offers, or fake prizes
