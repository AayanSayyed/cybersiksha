🔐 Learn to Surf the Web Anonymously

In this simulation, you'll learn how to protect your identity online by using VPNs, the Tor network, secure search engines, and privacy-focused habits. Before doing anything, try making a search and see how traceable you are!

Step 1: Use a VPN

A VPN (Virtual Private Network) hides your real IP address and encrypts your internet traffic. This makes your connection more private and secure.

Step 2: Use Tor Browser

Tor is a browser that bounces your traffic through several nodes. It hides your real location and helps bypass censorship. But it’s slower than normal browsing.

Step 3: Use Privacy Search Engines

Use DuckDuckGo or Startpage instead of Google. They don’t track you or sell your search history.

Step 4: Block Trackers

Install browser extensions like uBlock Origin or Privacy Badger to block ads and tracking scripts that spy on your browsing behavior.

Step 5: Good Habits = Privacy

Use fake names, temporary emails, and never overshare. Your behavior matters more than you think!
